VIRAMAYAK'26 Messages & Overview

විරාමයක්'26 - An Evening of Music, Rhythm & Memories

Organized with pride by the Students' Union, Faculty of Engineering, University of Sri Jayewardenepura. This grand musical initiative brings together students, alumni, and music lovers to celebrate rhythm, memories, and unity while championing the noble causes of Sith Paura.

Alumna – Faculty of Engineering, University of Sri Jayewardenepura

May this noble endeavour through Sith Paura open doors to brighter futures, inspire countless dreams, and remind us that even a little kindness can make a lasting difference. Wishing the organizing committee every success in creating a meaningful impact!"
